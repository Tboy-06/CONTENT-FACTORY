## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. There was a time when the mere mention of John D. Rockefeller could make the most powerful men in America tremble.
2. Not only was he the world's first billionaire, but he remains one of the wealthiest individuals in human history.
3. His empire, Standard Oil, ascended to absolute dominance by brutally crushing every rival in its path.
4. Rockefeller mastered multiple industries by dictating terms, manipulating logistics, and bribing governments into submission.
5. He approached commerce not as a fair exchange, but as total war.
6. Such ruthless tactics and secretive deals inevitably turned ruthless ambition into countless enemies.
7. Yet beneath the cold exterior of a ruthless monopolist lay a deeply complex titan who reshaped the modern economy.
8. Ultimately, Rockefeller's ruthless legacy proved that immense fortune is rarely built without immense cost.
