# Story Structure: How to write descriptively - Nalo Hopkinson

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 282.0s
- Hook: "We read fiction for many reasons."
- Payoff: "Her tongue is a bristly sponge, and her eyes are bags of bleach." Did that description almost make you feel as queasy as Billie?"

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. We read fiction for many reasons.
2. To be entertained, to find out who done it, to travel to strange, new planets, to be scared,
3. to laugh, to cry, to think, to feel, to be so absorbed that for a while we forget where we are.
4. So, how about writing fiction?
5. How do you suck your readers into your stories?
6. With an exciting plot? Maybe.
7. "Billie's legs are noodles. The ends of her hair are poison needles.
8. Her tongue is a bristly sponge, and her eyes are bags of bleach." Did that description almost make you feel as queasy as Billie?
