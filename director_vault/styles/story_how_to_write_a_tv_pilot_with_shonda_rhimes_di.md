## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. The path to writing a world-class television pilot begins long before you put pen to paper.
2. Shonda Rhimes suggests that your first step is to aggressively curate a collection of the best pilot scripts in the industry.
3. Identify the shows you personally love and challenge yourself to imagine how you would have crafted their opening episodes.
4. Because these scripts are now widely accessible, there is no excuse for failing to study the blueprints of successful storytelling.
5. Begin your masterclass by reading a script, then watch the corresponding episode to see how those words translated to the screen.
6. Go back to the page and read the script once more to uncover the subtle choices that turned a simple idea into a hit.
7. Start to systematically dissect the narrative structure and character beats that give these pilots their undeniable power.
8. By reverse-engineering the work of the greats, you move from merely watching television to understanding the mechanics of how it is built.
