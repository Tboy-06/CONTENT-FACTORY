## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. I just lost 300 million in the blink of an eye, but instead of dying, I woke up exactly ten years in the past.
2. It is September 2014, and I am standing in my freshman classroom as if the tragedy never happened.
3. The students around me are whispering, mocking my dazed expression and lack of sophistication.
4. They have no idea that the man standing before them holds the roadmap to the next decade of global wealth.
5. As the welcome meeting begins, I walk to the front of the room with a calm, predatory efficiency.
6. My introduction is short: no hobbies, no vices, just a singular, relentless focus on making money.
7. With only a few thousand dollars in my pocket and the blueprints of the future in my head, the game is already rigged in my favor.
8. In a world where everyone else is chasing grades, I am playing a game they cannot even see.
