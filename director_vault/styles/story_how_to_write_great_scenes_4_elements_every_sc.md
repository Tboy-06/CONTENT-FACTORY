## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Some scenes linger in our minds forever through the sheer magnetism of an unforgettable performance.
2. Others achieve cinematic immortality through a breathtaking visual shot or a perfectly placed song.
3. While writing great cinema resists a rigid formula, certain structural pillars reliably point you in the right direction.
4. Every truly great scene must possess a clear objective that drives the characters forward against formidable resistance.
5. Conflict arises naturally when opposing forces collide, transforming a simple conversation into a high-stakes battleground.
6. A successful scene must also shift the dramatic landscape, ensuring the characters are different at the end than they were at the start.
7. Mastering these foundational elements allows you to construct narratives that captivate audiences from the very first frame to the final cut.
8. Great screenwriting is ultimately the art of engineering purposeful change through conflict.
