## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Tucked deep within your chest, a relentless engine beats with rhythmic precision to sustain your very existence.
2. The heart functions as a biological pump, tirelessly driving oxygen and essential nutrients to every corner of the body.
3. This constant circulation of blood is the fundamental pillar of human life.
4. Structurally, the heart is a marvel of engineering, composed of robust muscle layers designed to withstand a lifetime of exertion.
5. The thin exterior pericardium shields the organ, while the delicate endocardium lines its interior walls.
6. Beneath this tough exterior, the heart is divided into four distinct chambers that work in perfect synchronization.
7. The two upper chambers, or atria, act as receiving docks for incoming blood before passing it to the powerful lower ventricles.
8. Ultimately, this tireless mechanical process is what fuels the billions of cells that make you who you are.
