2. This massive disparity reveals that wealth isn't just a binary choice between rich and poor, but a spectrum with distinct, life-altering tiers.
3. We are peeling back the curtain on the five levels of wealth that define the modern global elite.
4. At the entry level, the focus is on financial security and freedom from the daily grind of survival.
5. As we climb, we encounter the affluent who leverage capital to reshape their entire reality.
6. Yet, the gap between the millionaire and the billionaire is not merely quantitative; it is a fundamental shift in influence, reach, and power.
7. We will explore how the ultra-rich navigate a world that operates by an entirely different set of economic rules.
