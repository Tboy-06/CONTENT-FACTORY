# Story Structure: Script Formatting in Google Docs - Short and Simple - 2019

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 161.0s
- Hook: "Professional screenwriting demands a specific look, but you can master the craft in Google Docs with just a few simple rules."
- Payoff: "Consistency is the secret to appearing professional in a competitive industry where formatting details truly matter."

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Professional screenwriting demands a specific look, but you can master the craft in Google Docs with just a few simple rules.
2. First, your font choice is non-negotiable; always set your text to Courier New at a 12-point size.
3. This classic font is the industry standard because it ensures that one page of text reliably equals one minute of screen time.
4. Next, every new scene begins with a slug line, which serves as a vital anchor for your reader.
5. You must write this line entirely in capital letters to distinguish it from the rest of the body text.
6. Start by identifying the setting as either interior or exterior, followed by the specific name of your location.
7. By sticking to these rigid formatting conventions, you ensure your screenplay remains clean, readable, and ready for production.
8. Consistency is the secret to appearing professional in a competitive industry where formatting details truly matter.
