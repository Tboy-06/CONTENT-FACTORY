## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. If you want your play to land on a stage instead of the trash bin, you must master the art of professional formatting.
2. Submitting a script to a theater company requires more than just a great story; it demands a standard level of industry readiness.
3. When your document follows established conventions, readers immediately take your work and your voice seriously.
4. Proper formatting ensures that busy artistic directors focus on your narrative arc rather than deciphering a messy page layout.
5. Start by ensuring every line of dialogue is indented and clearly separated from the surrounding text.
6. Always place your stage directions in italics to create a sharp, unmistakable visual distinction from the spoken word.
7. Keep your page design clean and spacious, resisting the urge to cram too much text into a single scene.
8. By perfecting these technical basics, you guarantee that your creative feedback centers on the heart of the play rather than administrative errors.
