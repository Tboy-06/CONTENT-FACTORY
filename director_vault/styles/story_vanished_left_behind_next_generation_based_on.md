4. Life used to be simple, beautiful, and filled with a future that seemed guaranteed to unfold exactly as we planned.
5. Now, those memories feel like a cruel ghost haunting the wreckage of a civilization that vanished in the blink of an eye.
6. We are trapped in a bleak reality, endlessly fixing broken things while living lives that no longer feel like our own.
7. The weight of this abandonment forces us to confront the terrifying question of why we were the ones left behind.
8. In the silence of this new age, we must decide if we will simply survive or finally find a purpose in the ruins.
