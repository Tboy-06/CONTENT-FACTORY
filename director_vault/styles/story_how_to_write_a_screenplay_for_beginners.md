## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Screenwriting is often considered the most formidable challenge in the entire filmmaking process.
2. While the internet is flooded with complex storytelling theory, the truth is that every great project begins with a simple grasp of the fundamentals.
3. I have spent years crafting hundreds of sketches, dozens of short films, and several feature-length projects for major production companies.
4. Despite this extensive professional experience, I still approach every new script as a student of the craft.
5. My goal today is not to dictate a single correct way to write, but to distill the essential 101 principles that will get you started.
6. We will strip away the intimidating industry jargon to focus on the core mechanics that turn a blank page into a living story.
7. By mastering these foundational elements, you can stop feeling overwhelmed and start putting your unique vision onto the screen.
8. Remember that even the most seasoned veterans are constantly learning, so your journey begins simply by writing your first word.
