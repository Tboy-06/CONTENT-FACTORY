## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. The moment you started reading this sentence, you triggered a complex neurological phenomenon that defies the laws of human evolution.
2. While we often assume reading is a natural extension of our spoken language, it is actually a profound biological anomaly.
3. Your brain arrived equipped with dedicated circuitry for sight and sound, but it lacks any innate architecture for interpreting written symbols.
4. Reading is a revolutionary invention, requiring your mind to repurpose ancient neural pathways never intended for literacy.
5. Scientific evidence confirms that your brain undergoes physical rewiring the moment you begin to decipher characters on a page.
6. This process forces distinct regions of the brain to collaborate in ways that our ancestors never required for survival.
7. In the grand timeline of human history, literacy is a blink-of-an-eye development that reshapes your cognitive identity.
8. By learning to read, you are effectively hijacking your own evolution to expand the limits of what a human mind can achieve.
