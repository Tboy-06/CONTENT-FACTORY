## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Clicking on this video activated circuits in your brain that took thousands of years to develop the ones required for reading.
2. We think of language as natural and reading is written language, so it must be natural. But it isn't.
3. It isn't natural at all.
4. Scientific studies indicate that a neurotypical brain is born with the circuitry that allows our eyes to see
5. and our vocal cords to produce sounds, but it doesn't innately have the ability to read.
6. From an evolutionary time scale, our brain hasn't had enough time to develop a dedicated reading brain.
7. And so to build a reading brain network, we co-opt parts of the brain involved in vision and auditory processing,
8. and language and attention and affect.
