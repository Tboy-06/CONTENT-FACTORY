## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Most creators wait until they reach their destination to figure out what they are filming, but that is the fastest way to lose your audience's attention.
2. Early in my journey, I subscribed to the daily vlog style, simply pressing record on whatever happened to unfold in front of me.
3. While those early videos acted as a charming slice of time, they lacked the narrative backbone necessary to sustain long-term engagement.
4. I eventually realized that capturing raw events is not the same as crafting a compelling story.
5. You must decide what you are going to shoot long before you pick up the camera.
6. Transitioning from documenting life to scripting ideas changed everything about how my videos connect with viewers.
7. A clear vision ensures that every clip serves a specific purpose, turning disjointed footage into a cohesive narrative experience.
8. Stop chasing the moment and start designing the story if you want your content to truly resonate.
