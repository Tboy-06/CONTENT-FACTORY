## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 2019 alone, Red Bull sold nearly one can for every human on Earth, turning a sugary caffeine kick into a global obsession.
2. Beyond the 7.5 billion cans sold, the company functions as a sprawling, high-octane media empire.
3. They own two Formula One teams, five professional football clubs, and an international roster of elite athletes.
4. From death-defying Crashed Ice challenges to the charitable Wings for Life Run, their brand footprint is everywhere.
5. They produce professional-grade media content that rivals major television networks, shifting their identity from a beverage company to an entertainment powerhouse.
6. While it looks like a chaotic collection of expensive hobbies, every dollar spent is a calculated move to dominate the attention economy.
7. Critics often ask if this massive portfolio is just elaborate marketing designed to justify a premium price tag on a cheap liquid.
8. Red Bull has mastered the art of selling a lifestyle instead of a drink, proving that they are not just in the beverage business, but in the business of pure adrenaline.
