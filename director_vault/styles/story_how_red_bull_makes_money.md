## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 2019, Red Bull sold nearly one can of their sweet energy drink for every single person on the planet.
2. Yet, moving an astonishing 7.5 billion cans is only a fraction of their actual empire.
3. Beneath the surface of this beverage giant lies ownership of two Formula One teams, five professional football clubs, and an ice hockey team.
4. They also fund grueling extreme sports spectacles like the crashed ice challenge and the global Wings for Life run.
5. Thousands of elite athletes wear the iconic charging bulls, backed by massive sponsorship deals and high-end media production houses.
6. Casual observers might assume this sprawling universe of adrenaline is nothing more than a glorified marketing campaign.
7. However, this relentless cultural saturation forms a brilliant economic engine designed to feed the bottom line.
8. Ultimately, Red Bull proves that by selling a lifestyle instead of just a beverage, you can conquer both the sports world and the global economy.
