### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc

2. He famously dismisses the practice of 'idea worship,' arguing that chasing a singular good idea is a fundamental mistake for any writer.
3. When a writer suggested the absurd premise of Rick turning himself into a pickle, Harmon didn't see a joke; he saw a narrative puzzle.
4. He immediately pivoted to the challenge of justifying such an innocuous action through the most complex, character-driven psychology possible.
5. For Harmon, television functions primarily as a comfort tool, necessitating a grounded connection that transcends the surface-level madness of the plot.
6. By deconstructing characters like Jeff Winger, he proves that even the most cynical archetypes must be built from relatable, human parts.
7. True writing magic happens when you stop obsessing over the quality of your initial concept and start interrogating the character's motivation.
8. Ultimately, the secret to Harmon's success is finding profound depth within the most ridiculous, throwaway ideas.
