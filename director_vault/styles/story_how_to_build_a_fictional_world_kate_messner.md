## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Every unforgettable fictional universe is built upon a foundation of strict internal logic and deep history.
2. Master world-builders like J.R.R. Tolkien understood that even immortal beings must operate within defined physical and spiritual boundaries.
3. In Middle Earth, Gandalf possesses a mortal body subject to worldly perils, yet retains an immortal spirit capable of resurrection.
4. This careful balancing act between the ordinary and the extraordinary grounds fantastical elements in a believable reality.
5. Similarly, the Wachowskis constructed a dystopian digital realm where awakened humans hack the neon binary code to instantly acquire complex skills like flying a helicopter.
6. Whether you are crafting ancient fantasy or futuristic cyberpunk, your setting requires rules that dictate what is possible.
7. When characters must navigate these established limitations, the fictional world instantly feels authentic and alive to the audience.
8. Ultimately, great world-building is not just about creating imagination, but about maintaining absolute consistency within the realities you invent.
