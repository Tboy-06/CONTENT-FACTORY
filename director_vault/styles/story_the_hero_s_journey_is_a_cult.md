## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. We treat the Hero’s Journey like a blueprint for living, but we fail to see it for what it truly is: the bedrock of a modern-day cult.
2. My own decade spent managing an online community has shown me how quickly an idle joke can metastasize into an unquestioning, radicalized following.
3. I still remember when a lazy renaming of my religion to the 'boat Mormons' during a game of Civ 5 spiraled into a persistent, bizarre obsession among my followers.
4. To this day, my Discord server remains a daily testing ground for people desperately searching for a dogma to anchor their aimless lives.
5. When we view our own existence through the lens of a singular narrative, we stop being individuals and start becoming recruits.
6. This phenomenon isn't limited to niche corners of the internet; we see it play out on a national stage whenever a charismatic figure offers a clear path to greatness.
7. Confused people crave a story where they are the protagonists, even if that story is just a vehicle for someone else's control.
8. The Hero’s Journey is not just a template for storytelling; it is a psychological trap that invites us to surrender our autonomy to a script we didn't write.
