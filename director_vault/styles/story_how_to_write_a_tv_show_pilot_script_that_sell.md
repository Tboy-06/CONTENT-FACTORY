## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Most pilot scripts fail because they feel like a random sequence of events rather than a cohesive story.
2. We have all sat through those agonizing episodes where the plot drifts aimlessly and fails to hold our attention.
3. To sell a project, your pilot must move with relentless momentum from the very first page.
4. The secret to this structural mastery lies in a simple, transformative rule developed by South Park creators Trey Parker and Matt Stone.
5. Many writers mistakenly connect their scenes with "and then," leading to a narrative that lacks genuine consequence.
6. Instead, your plot points must be linked by "therefore" and "but" to ensure every action forces a reaction.
7. By tightening your cause-and-effect chain, you transform a series of disconnected ideas into a compelling, engine-driven pilot.
8. Master the art of logical progression to ensure your script creates the unstoppable momentum that industry executives are desperate to buy.
