## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. To understand why Steve Jobs achieved the impossible, you must first accept that he was not a nice guy.
2. Working alongside industry titans taught me that the difference between mediocrity and greatness boils down to a single metric: the signal-to-noise ratio.
3. Most people spend their waking hours distracted by the static of trivial concerns, office politics, and irrelevant goals.
4. Jobs, however, refused to deal with noise, choosing instead to operate with ruthless, laser-focused intent.
5. For the eighteen hours he was awake every single day, his mind remained one hundred percent signal.
6. He filtered out everything that did not directly serve his vision, effectively purging his life of all secondary distractions.
7. If you want to replicate his monumental success, you must learn to aggressively cut the noise that clutters your own potential.
8. True mastery is not found in doing more, but in the relentless elimination of everything that is not the signal.
