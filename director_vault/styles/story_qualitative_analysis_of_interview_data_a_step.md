## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Turning raw interview transcripts into meaningful insights is a methodical art form that requires patience and precision.
2. Begin your journey by performing a rapid, high-level browse of all your transcripts to capture the overarching narrative landscape.
3. Jot down your initial impressions immediately, as these gut instincts often illuminate the most significant patterns in your data.
4. Transition from the bird’s-eye view to an immersive, deep-dive phase where you read each transcript line by line with absolute focus.
5. Once you have internalized the content, initiate the coding process by isolating and labeling specific words, phrases, or sentences.
6. Think of these labels as anchors that define the core actions, activities, or concepts emerging from the voices of your participants.
7. By systematically tagging these segments, you transform a disorganized mountain of text into a structured, analytical framework.
8. Mastering this rigorous indexing process is the critical step that bridges the gap between chaotic conversation and profound research discovery.
