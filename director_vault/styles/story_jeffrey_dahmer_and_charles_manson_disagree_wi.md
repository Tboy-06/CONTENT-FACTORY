4. He sat mere inches from his interviewer, shifting the blame entirely onto the dark desires that consumed his mind.
5. Yet, behind prison walls, not everyone agreed with Bundy's self-serving psychological autopsy.
6. Men like Jeffrey Dahmer and Charles Manson walked different paths to madness, often rejecting the tidy narratives spun by their infamous peers.
7. Dahmer blamed isolation and internal decay, while Manson preached apocalyptic cult philosophy rather than modern media consumption.
8. Ultimately, Bundy's desperate attempt to externalize his monstrous guilt remains one of the most chilling deceptions in criminal history.
