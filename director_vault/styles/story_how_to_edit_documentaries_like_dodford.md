## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Retention-based editing isn't about how fast you click, but how effectively your cuts manipulate the viewer's emotions.
2. Most editors treat the craft like a mechanical chore, forgetting that true storytelling requires building context that forces the audience to feel something.
3. Take, for example, the way we can transform a viewer's prejudice against someone like Adam Sandler simply by reframing their past experiences.
4. When you provide the right context, you bridge the gap between abstract visuals and raw human shame.
5. I once took a static image of the back of a woman's head and digitally extended her hair to create a seamless bridge into her real-life stage entrance.
6. By manipulating the visual narrative in this specific way, the transition between the edit and reality becomes invisible.
7. You have to be willing to kill your darlings, discarding what you don't like to make room for the footage you actually crave.
8. Mastering the edit means choosing the precise moment to shift perspective, ultimately turning a standard sequence into an unforgettable experience.
