## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 1957, while McDonald’s was aggressively dominating the market, a desperate Florida startup named Insta-Burger stood on the razor’s edge of total collapse.
2. This failing venture was drowning in debt and seemingly destined to vanish into fast-food obscurity.
3. Yet, through a series of radical pivots and calculated risks, this bankrupt enterprise would eventually morph into the global juggernaut we know as Burger King.
4. Today, the brand commands tens of billions in annual revenue, operating thousands of locations across more than a hundred countries.
5. The journey to this empire, however, is far from a standard business success story.
6. At one pivotal moment, the company’s survival required the frantic destruction of their own equipment with a literal axe.
7. Behind the familiar flame-grilled logo lies a chaotic history defined by high-stakes gambles and near-death experiences.
8. This is the untold truth of how a dying business dismantled its own foundation to reinvent the world of fast food.
