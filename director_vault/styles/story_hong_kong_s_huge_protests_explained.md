## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Hundreds of thousands of people flood the streets of Hong Kong, united in fierce opposition to a deeply unpopular government bill.
2. However, this unprecedented uprising quickly evolves into a much larger struggle over sovereignty and the encroaching power of mainland China.
3. Citizens are fighting desperately to preserve the unique civil liberties and freedoms that define their autonomous home.
4. Behind this massive political explosion lies a grim and tragic catalyst.
5. On February 8, 2018, a young couple named Chan Tong Kai and Poon Hiu-Wing leave Hong Kong for a holiday in Taiwan.
6. They check into the Purple Garden Hotel, unaware that their journey will alter the course of history.
7. A brutal murder shatters the quiet trip, exposing a dangerous legal loophole between jurisdictions.
8. This single crime inadvertently triggers a seismic geopolitical battle that shakes the global stage.
