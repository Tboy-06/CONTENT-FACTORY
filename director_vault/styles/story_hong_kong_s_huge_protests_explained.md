## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. The streets of Hong Kong are flooding with hundreds of thousands of protesters, signaling a seismic shift in the city's relationship with China.
2. While the demonstrations were triggered by a deeply unpopular legislative bill, the unrest represents a much larger struggle for autonomy.
3. At the heart of this conflict is a desperate fight to preserve the unique freedoms that define life in the territory.
4. The catalyst for this entire movement was a brutal, tragic crime that occurred far from the city's political centers.
5. In February 2018, a young couple traveled from Hong Kong to Taiwan for what was supposed to be a peaceful vacation.
6. When only one of them returned, the ensuing legal deadlock exposed deep flaws in the extradition laws between the two regions.
7. The government attempted to bridge this legal gap with a new bill, but citizens feared it would be used as a tool for political overreach.
8. What began as a pursuit of justice for a murder victim quickly transformed into a landmark uprising against encroaching authoritarian power.
