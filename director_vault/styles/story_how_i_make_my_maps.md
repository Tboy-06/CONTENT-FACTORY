## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Every polished cartography video you admire today hides a deeply humbling beginning.
2. My own map-making journey did not start with slick animations or professional software.
3. Back in 2012, working as a foreign service officer, I created my very first map.
4. Looking back at that early work physically pains me, especially hearing my forced, casual voice trying to sound experienced.
5. At just twenty-four years old, I realized I had a massive amount to learn.
6. Instead of staying discouraged by my terrible results, I poured myself into mastering After Effects.
7. Transformation does not happen overnight, but rather through countless hours of dedicated practice.
8. You simply have to start somewhere if you ever want to reach your destination.
