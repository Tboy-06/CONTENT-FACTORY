## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. If you possess a functional human brain, you are already a master storyteller.
2. While this claim may sound like the hyperbole of a typical TED Talk, the reality is rooted in our evolutionary history.
3. For millions of years, our minds have been precisely engineered to craft and process narratives.
4. Storytelling is not a rare talent reserved for authors, but a fundamental biological mechanism within us all.
5. Every moment of your waking life is spent constructing a subjective reality through the lens of narrative.
6. Your brain is constantly interpreting the world, organizing disparate events into a meaningful sequence.
7. By understanding the science behind this internal process, you can harness your innate ability to captivate any audience.
8. You do not need to learn how to tell a story because you have been living one since the day you were born.
