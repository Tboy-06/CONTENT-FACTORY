## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 479 BC, the retreating sea offered Persian soldiers a lethal trap disguised as a shortcut.
2. As the invaders marched across the exposed seabed to siege the city of Potidaea, the ocean suddenly surged back with unprecedented fury.
3. Ancient witnesses credited the wrath of Poseidon, but modern science reveals a far more mechanical culprit: the tsunami.
4. These massive waves are not typically caused by tides or wind, but by the violent displacement of water from deep-sea disturbances.
5. Earthquakes, landslides, or volcanic eruptions act like a giant piston, shoving colossal volumes of ocean upward.
6. Across the deep, open sea, these waves move at the speed of a jet plane, often passing unnoticed beneath passing ships.
7. However, as they approach the shallow coastline, the water slows and compresses, forcing the wave to tower into a destructive wall of energy.
8. Understanding this deadly physics is the only way to transform a terrifying natural mystery into a manageable survival reality.
