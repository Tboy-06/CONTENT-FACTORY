# Story Structure: Escaping the Rat Race: What School Failed to Teach You About Money.

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 1059.0s
- Hook: "In 2003, heavyweight champion Mike Tyson shocked the world by filing for bankruptcy with thirty million dollars in debt."
- Payoff: "Escaping the rat race requires recognizing that true financial freedom is not about making more money, but mastering the art of keeping it."

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 2003, heavyweight champion Mike Tyson shocked the world by filing for bankruptcy with thirty million dollars in debt.
2. This stunning financial collapse happened despite him accumulating over three hundred million dollars during his legendary career.
3. His downfall forces us to confront an uncomfortable truth about the fundamental nature of money itself.
4. From the moment we wake up to the jobs we work, our entire lives are driven by the underlying desire to acquire wealth.
5. Yet, chasing a bigger paycheck becomes entirely pointless if we never learn how to actually manage what we make.
6. Traditional education leaves a massive blind spot, preparing us to earn a living rather than how to preserve it.
7. Today, modern consumers are trapped in this exact cycle, pushing American credit card debt to unprecedented all-time highs.
8. Escaping the rat race requires recognizing that true financial freedom is not about making more money, but mastering the art of keeping it.
