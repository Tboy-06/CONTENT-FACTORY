## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Imagine building a company that everyone thought would change the world, only to watch it become one of the biggest frauds in history.
2. Theranos began in 2003 as a startup of almost mythical proportions and ambitious goals.
3. At the helm was a woman once hailed as a visionary genius who would revolutionize modern healthcare.
4. Instead of delivering the medical miracle she promised, Elizabeth Holmes constructed an elaborate house of cards.
5. Investors and patients alike bought into the illusion, believing a single drop of blood could diagnose countless diseases.
6. Behind closed doors, the technology failed repeatedly while executives spun elaborate webs of deception to hide the truth.
7. The inevitable collapse transformed a Silicon Valley fairy tale into a sobering federal criminal case.
8. Ultimately, the Theranos saga serves as an incredible morality tale about the dangerous cost of unchecked ambition.
