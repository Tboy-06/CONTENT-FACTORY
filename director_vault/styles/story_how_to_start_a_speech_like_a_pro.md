# Story Structure: How to start a speech like a PRO

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 507.0s
- Hook: "You have exactly one job in the first thirty seconds of any speech, pitch, or meeting: force your audience to sit up and listen."
- Payoff: "Stop waiting for people to care and start crafting a beginning that makes it impossible for them to look away."

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. You have exactly one job in the first thirty seconds of any speech, pitch, or meeting: force your audience to sit up and listen.
2. Do not fall into the trap of believing that your audience owes you their undivided attention.
3. It is not their responsibility to focus, but rather your sole responsibility to capture that focus immediately.
4. Most presentations fail before they truly begin because their openings are as dull as dishwater.
5. This inevitable boredom is why your audience reaches for their phones, checks their emails, or starts side conversations.
6. When you start with predictable filler, you surrender control of the room and lose your influence instantly.
7. The good news is that you can break this cycle of indifference with a single, calculated choice.
8. Stop waiting for people to care and start crafting a beginning that makes it impossible for them to look away.
