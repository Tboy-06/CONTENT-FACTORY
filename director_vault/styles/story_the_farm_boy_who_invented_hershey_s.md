## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 1881, a disgraced young man walked away from his family home with nothing but a broken spirit and a pocket full of empty promises.
2. After two consecutive business failures, Milton Hershey stood before his uncles, begging for one final chance to prove his worth.
3. Instead of an investment, he was met with biting insults and a cold eviction from the family estate.
4. At that time, chocolate was an elite status symbol, a decadent luxury strictly reserved for the wealthiest members of society.
5. Driven by the sting of rejection, Hershey refused to let his past failures define his future.
6. He embarked on a relentless mission to transform that rare, expensive treat into an affordable comfort for every common person.
7. By pioneering new manufacturing techniques, he built an empire that turned a simple cocoa bean into a global phenomenon.
8. Milton Hershey proved that the greatest empires are often built on the foundation of our most bitter defeats.
