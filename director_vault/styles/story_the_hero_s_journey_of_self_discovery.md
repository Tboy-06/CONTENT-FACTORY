### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Deep within the ancient stories of our ancestors lies a hidden blueprint for the modern human experience.
2. Jonathan Young, a psychologist and curator of the Joseph Campbell Archive, has spent a lifetime decoding this mythic imagination.
3. His work reveals how the psychological struggles we face today mirror the epic trials found in age-old narratives.
4. Guided by the legendary existentialist Rollo May, Young sought to bridge the gap between clinical practice and the power of myth.
5. The trajectory of his life shifted profoundly when a chance encounter brought him into the inner circle of Joseph Campbell himself.
6. Through these mythological texts, we begin to understand that our personal challenges are actually steps in a universal heroic journey.
7. By examining these archetypal patterns, we gain the clarity needed to navigate our own paths toward self-discovery.
8. Ultimately, we learn that by embracing our own inner myths, we transform the story of our lives into something truly heroic.
