# Story Structure: Kurt Vonnegut on how to write a short story

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 89.0s
- Hook: "Kurt Vonnegut once offered a masterclass in fiction that stripped the art of storytelling down to its brutal, essential bones."
- Payoff: "Ultimately, writing a great story is not about ornamentation, but about the rigorous, uncompromising pursuit of human truth."

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Kurt Vonnegut once offered a masterclass in fiction that stripped the art of storytelling down to its brutal, essential bones.
2. First, you must treat your reader’s time as a sacred resource that can never be squandered.
3. Second, ensure there is at least one character in your narrative whom the audience can genuinely root for.
4. Third, every character must harbor a distinct desire, even if that motivation is as small as a glass of water.
5. Fourth, demand efficiency by making every single sentence either reveal a character’s soul or advance the plot.
6. Fifth, structure your narrative by starting as close to the climactic end as possible.
7. Sixth, be a sadist to your characters regardless of how sweet or innocent they may appear.
8. Ultimately, writing a great story is not about ornamentation, but about the rigorous, uncompromising pursuit of human truth.
