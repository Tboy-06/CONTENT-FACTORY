## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. It is the most common job interview question of all time, usually deployed as the very first icebreaker when you sit across the desk.
2. While a million different YouTube videos offer a million different ways to tackle it, the sheer volume of advice often creates paralyzing confusion.
3. Most candidates ramble through their life story, losing the room within the first thirty seconds.
4. To master this open-ended trap, we must completely reverse our perspective and look at the dynamic from the other side of the table.
5. A former CEO brings a ruthlessly pragmatic lens to the hiring process, stripping away the nervous fluff.
6. The secret lies not in telling them who you are, but in immediately proving how your background solves their specific pain point.
7. By keeping your introduction remarkably quick, simple, and targeted, you instantly command respect and set a confident tone.
8. Mastering this single framework transforms an awkward icebreaker into your ultimate competitive advantage.
