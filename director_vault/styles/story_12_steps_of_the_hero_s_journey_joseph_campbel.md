## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Deep within the blueprint of every legendary tale lies a universal structure known as the Hero’s Journey.
2. Coined by Joseph Campbell in 1949, this monomyth suggests that across all cultures, great stories share a singular, recurring DNA.
3. Disney’s Moana serves as the perfect modern vessel to examine these ancient storytelling beats in action.
4. The journey ignites in the Ordinary World of Motunui, where Moana feels the tug of a call she cannot yet name.
5. After refusing the call, she ultimately accepts her destiny, crossing the threshold into the vast, unforgiving ocean.
6. Through a series of trials, she encounters mentors and enemies, facing the internal and external battles required to forge a true hero.
7. She eventually reaches the innermost cave, confronting the fire demon Te Kā to restore the heart of Te Fiti.
8. By returning home transformed, Moana proves that the hero's journey is not just about the destination, but the profound evolution of the self.
