## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Imagine redesigning education to become as dangerously addictive as a social media feed.
2. Luis von Ahn, the visionary behind Duolingo, began his journey far from the tech hubs of Silicon Valley.
3. Hailing from Guatemala—a nation he humorously clarified is neither Guantanamo nor Mexico's prison system—he understood the barriers of global inequality firsthand.
4. Growing up just south of the border, he observed complex socioeconomic dynamics that shaped his worldview on access and opportunity.
5. He realized that traditional education systems were failing because they lacked the compulsive engagement loops of modern technology.
6. By weaponizing psychological triggers, streaks, and gamification, his platform transformed the grueling chore of studying into a compulsive daily habit.
7. Millions of users now log on compulsively, trading mindless scrolling for language acquisition without ever feeling like they are studying.
8. Ultimately, von Ahn proved that when you make learning as irresistible as entertainment, human potential unlocks itself.
