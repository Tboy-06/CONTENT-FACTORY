## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. On the edge of the deep blue sea, a simple fisherman and his discontented wife lived in a tiny, crumbling cottage.
2. Every day, the fisherman cast his line into the rhythmic waves, finding peace in the humble rhythm of the tides.
3. While he was perfectly content with his modest catch, his wife viewed their damp, moldy home with growing bitterness.
4. She loathed the constant cleaning and dreamed of a life far grander than their peeling walls could ever provide.
5. Though the fisherman loved her deeply, his best efforts to please her were met with persistent, insatiable complaints.
6. If he brought home a fresh fish, she demanded a crab, highlighting an emptiness no meal could ever satisfy.
7. This classic fable masterfully illustrates how unchecked greed can slowly poison the serenity of a quiet life.
8. Ultimately, the story serves as a timeless warning that true happiness cannot be caught in a net, but must be found within the heart.
