## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Before you write a single page of your screenplay, you must survive a brutal interrogation by the foundational mechanics of narrative.
2. Veteran screenwriter Glenn Gers insists that if you cannot definitively answer six specific questions, you simply do not have a story yet.
3. At the very heart of this framework lies a deceptively simple definition: following a character through a journey of change.
4. Many writers mistakenly believe this requires a lonely single protagonist carrying the entire emotional weight of the film.
5. In reality, the master storyteller knows it is entirely possible to weave a compelling tapestry featuring a dozen main characters.
6. Managing a sprawling ensemble demands immense structural discipline because you must meticulously track the arc of every single individual.
7. Without that rigorous tracking, your sprawling narrative immediately collapses into a chaotic mess of disconnected scenes.
8. Mastering these essential questions transforms a confusing jumble of ideas into a tightly focused, unforgettable cinematic experience.
