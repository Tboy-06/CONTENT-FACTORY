## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. When Joseph Romm was twenty-four years old, an obsession with language led him away from the rigorous halls of MIT straight to the birthplace of Shakespeare.
2. Armed with a deep fascination for the Elizabethan playwright, he traveled to Stratford-upon-Avon to immerse himself in the immortal words that had captured his imagination.
3. Along the way, he encountered the sharp wit of a local joke highlighting the perceived supremacy of the Queen's English over its American counterpart.
4. Yet, his true pilgrimage was not about linguistic rivalry, but about experiencing the pinnacle of theatrical performance live on stage.
5. He had come to witness the Royal Shakespeare Company bring the dark machinations of Richard III to life with breathtaking mastery.
6. To devotees of classic English literature, this premier troupe functioned as the ultimate tribute band, executing every syllable with precision.
7. Mesmerized by their dedication, he followed the company from venue to venue, absorbing the profound mechanics of how stories are effectively transmitted.
8. This formative cultural immersion ultimately revealed that the foundational ingredients of powerful storytelling transcend mere words and connect directly to the human soul.
