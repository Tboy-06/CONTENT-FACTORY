## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Every great mystery starts with a single, dangerous thought that lures the reader into a trap of their own curiosity.
2. Bestselling author Holly Jackson knows exactly how to hook an audience, turning late-night reading sessions into an obsession.
3. Her process begins not with a plot, but with a spark of inspiration often found in the most mundane moments of daily life.
4. While walking her dog and deep into a true crime podcast, a simple question about case naming conventions shifted into a new story idea.
5. She realized that the power of a title like 'The Reappearance of Rachel Price' could serve as the ultimate narrative anchor.
6. Writing a thriller is more than just stacking clues; it is a calculated science of manipulating the reader's pace.
7. By mastering the art of the 'just one more chapter' hook, Jackson keeps her audience ensnared until the final truth is revealed.
8. Ultimately, the secret to a gripping thriller is crafting a story so compelling that the reader is powerless to walk away.
