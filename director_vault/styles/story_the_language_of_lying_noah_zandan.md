- Total Duration: 342.0s

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. We encounter anywhere from ten to two hundred lies every single day.
2. Humanity has spent centuries inventing elaborate machinery to catch these deceptions.
3. Inventors have deployed everything from medieval torture devices to modern polygraphs.
4. Detectors have ranged from simple blood-pressure monitors to heavy electroencephalograms.
5. Despite all this heavy hardware, our biological lie detectors remain remarkably flawed.
6. Instead of looking at machines, linguistic researchers began studying the actual structure of deceit.
7. Liars subconsciously distance themselves from their falsehoods by altering their language patterns.
8. By analyzing the mechanics of speech itself, we finally learn to decode the true language of lying.
