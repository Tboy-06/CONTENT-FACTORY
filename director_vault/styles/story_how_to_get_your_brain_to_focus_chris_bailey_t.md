## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Imagine waking up and instantly handing the keys to your mind over to a glowing piece of glass.
2. For productivity expert Chris Bailey, a startling realization emerged after he closely observed his own daily habits.
3. From the moment his eyes opened until the day ended, his life had quietly transformed into a relentless series of screens.
4. Morning routines began with the very device that woke him up, scrolling through endless cooking videos on Instagram.
5. Instead of starting the day with intention, his attention was hijacked by bouncing between a dozen different applications.
6. This modern trap of constant digital stimulation has fractured our collective ability to focus on what truly matters.
7. Reclaiming our concentration requires recognizing these invisible behavioral loops that quietly steal our time and energy.
8. By breaking the cycle of constant screen dependency, we can finally train our brains to focus again.
