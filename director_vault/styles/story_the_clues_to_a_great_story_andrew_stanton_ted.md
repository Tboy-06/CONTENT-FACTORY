## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Great stories are not just about what happens, but how they make us feel.
2. Andrew Stanton famously illustrates this by describing a tourist sharing a quiet, awkward pint in a lonely Scottish pub.
3. Suddenly, an old man shatters the silence with a bitter lament about the craftsmanship of the very bar they are leaning on.
4. The man recounts building the structure with his own hands, pouring more love into the wood than he did his own child.
5. Yet, he realizes that despite his lifelong dedication, the world defines him by his perceived failures rather than his greatest achievements.
6. This brief, stinging anecdote forces the listener to instantly empathize with a stranger’s hidden grief.
7. It proves that the best stories provide a framework for the audience to deduce the truth for themselves rather than being told it directly.
8. The secret to compelling storytelling is trusting your audience enough to let them fill in the emotional blanks.
