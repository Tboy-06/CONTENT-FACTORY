## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Great storytelling is not about giving the audience all the answers, but about making them care enough to put the pieces together themselves.
2. Master filmmaker Andrew Stanton reveals that the secret ingredient to any unforgettable narrative is active audience participation.
3. He illustrates this profound truth with the simple tale of a weary tourist stepping into a secluded Scottish pub.
4. Inside, the traveler finds only a quiet bartender and an elderly patron nursing a solitary beer in heavy silence.
5. Suddenly, the old man breaks the quiet and delivers a striking lament about the bar he built with his own hands.
6. He complains bitterly that despite pouring his entire life and soul into the wooden structure, nobody recognizes him as its creator.
7. Without finishing his thought, the man points dramatically out the window toward an entirely different landmark.
8. By purposefully withholding the punchline, the speaker forces our brains to instantly bridge the gap and co-create the joke.
