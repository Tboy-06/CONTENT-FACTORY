# Story Structure: Schooled by Kids: Presentation Skills, Part 1

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 105.0s
- Hook: "Sometimes the most profound lessons on public speaking come not from seasoned CEOs, but from the unfiltered wisdom of a child."
- Payoff: "True mastery of the stage comes down to removing distractions so your message can finally take center stage."

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Sometimes the most profound lessons on public speaking come not from seasoned CEOs, but from the unfiltered wisdom of a child.
2. Meet Kenneth, a young orator who believes the secret to a perfect TED-style talk begins the moment you step onto the stage.
3. His first rule of engagement is deceptively simple: walk in with unshakeable confidence and a genuine smile.
4. Without that warm expression, Kenneth warns that your audience will immediately wonder why you aren't enjoying yourself.
5. Physical presence is equally vital, requiring you to stand tall with your shoulders pulled back, yet balanced enough to avoid looking rigid.
6. Constant pacing is a common pitfall that can quickly fracture your audience's focus and derail your message.
7. If your hands feel awkward or restless, you must learn to anchor them firmly by your sides to maintain a steady visual connection.
8. True mastery of the stage comes down to removing distractions so your message can finally take center stage.
