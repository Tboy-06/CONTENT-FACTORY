## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Most universities teach writing from the bottom up, but the University of Chicago is doing things differently.
2. Across the nation, the standard academic model prioritizes the freshman experience through universal composition courses.
3. These programs focus on foundational mechanics, treating writing as a hurdle for entry-level students to clear.
4. The University of Chicago, however, rejects this conventional bottom-up approach in favor of a top-down philosophy.
5. By shifting the focus, they treat writing not as a basic requirement but as a sophisticated tool for advanced intellectual leadership.
6. This radical departure from the norm challenges students to prioritize clarity and intent long before they put pen to paper.
7. The result is a culture where effective communication is viewed as the ultimate expression of critical thinking.
8. Mastering the craft of writing requires moving beyond rote rules to embrace a design-first mindset that prioritizes the reader's understanding above all else.
