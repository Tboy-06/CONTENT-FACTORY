## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Modern storytelling has undergone a radical transformation, evolving into a vessel for ideological indoctrination rather than genuine human connection.
2. Across the landscape of contemporary cinema, we are witnessing the emergence of a parasitic trend where narrative tropes are hijacked to enforce specific social agendas.
3. This new paradigm fuses intersectional theory with revolutionary tropes to dismantle traditional structures and replace them with a curated, inverted version of reality.
4. Proponents of this movement view their work as a moral necessity, utilizing the reach of Hollywood to actively counteract historical patterns of exclusion and oppression.
5. However, this shift creates a parasitic relationship where the art itself serves as a mere host for a prescriptive, politically charged message.
6. By prioritizing ideological goals over the internal logic of a story, these films often fracture the audience’s suspension of disbelief.
7. We must ask whether this pursuit of social correction ultimately degrades the very medium it seeks to weaponize for change.
8. True artistry is being cannibalized by propaganda, leaving behind a hollow shell that serves an agenda rather than reflecting the complexities of the human condition.
