## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 1967, deep within the expanse of the cosmos, a regular, mechanical heartbeat began echoing across the void.
2. A brilliant Cambridge postgraduate student named Jocelyn Burnell was quietly surveying the sky using a newly constructed radio telescope.
3. After weeks of meticulous observation, her instruments captured a bizarre and unprecedented radio signal that appeared to be pulsating.
4. The mysterious pulses arrived at an interval of exactly 1.33 seconds, defying all known natural phenomena.
5. At first, exhausted researchers suspected the anomaly was nothing more than localized man-made interference cluttering the data.
6. However, rigorous analysis soon confirmed that the steady signal actually emanated from the darkest depths of deep space.
7. This unwavering precision sparked frantic theories about advanced extraterrestrial intelligence broadcasting across the galaxy.
8. In our relentless quest to conquer the great silence of the universe, we discovered that the stars themselves possess a voice.
