# Story Structure: Plot Mountain! | The Plot Diagram Song | Scratch Garden

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 24-Sentence 4-Act Documentary
- Total Duration: 170.0s
- Hook: "If you’re going to Plot Mountain
You gotta start somewhere"
- Payoff: "The next time on Plot Mountain!"

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Inciting Anomaly
1. If you’re going to Plot Mountain
You gotta start somewhere
2. We’ll call this the INTRODUCTION
And guess happens there?
3. You describe the characters and the settings
That’s the who, what, when, and where
4. The characters are the people that are there
What do they look like, why should we care?
5. And the setting is the place where they be
What exactly do the characters see?
6. On their way to Plot Mountain

### Act 2: Hidden Mechanism
1. There’s a conflict in the story
You should describe exactly what
2. Like a character has a problem to work through
With themselves or the world, what should
3. they do?
4. Or maybe there’s some big arguments
Or some dramatic natural events
5. On the way up Plot Mountain
6. Keep on going up Plot Mountain
Until you reach the top

### Act 3: Crisis/Climax
1. But action like this cannot stay
The story must change and go a different way
2. On the top of Plot Mountain
3. After the top of Plot Mountain
You’re going to start to go down
4. We call this the FALLING ACTION
And it lasts until flat ground
5. There’s still action in the story
But it will start to slow down
6. So the worst is over, the worst is through,
But now what will the characters do?

### Act 4: Aftermath
1. We call this the RESOLUTION
And what will happen there?
2. You should answer all the questions
Until you are aware:
3. That the conflicts in the story from before
Aren’t there any more
4. Unless there’s gonna be a sequel or part
2
5. And then you’ll have to think what will
the characters do?
6. The next time on Plot Mountain!
