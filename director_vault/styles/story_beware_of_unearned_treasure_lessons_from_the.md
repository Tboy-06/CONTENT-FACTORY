## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. We are often taught that happiness is a destination we reach by acquiring everything we desire, yet the greatest traps in life are the ones lined with unearned gold.
2. Consider the story of a prince born into a palace of infinite riches, where every whim was met with immediate, effortless surrender.
3. He demanded a soulmate, and she was delivered; he craved admiration, and the kingdom was forced to sing his praises; he desired wisdom, and every book in his library was rebranded to bear his name.
4. The prince lived a life of absolute ease, yet this hollow abundance stripped him of the only thing that truly matters: the growth found in the struggle.
5. True value cannot be bestowed upon us by others, nor can it be purchased through the convenience of a silver spoon.
6. As Paulo Coelho suggests in 'The Alchemist,' the journey toward our Personal Legend is meant to be forged in the fire of experience, not the comfort of entitlement.
7. When you possess everything without effort, you possess nothing of substance because you have never had to test the limits of your own spirit.
8. Ultimately, the treasure that is given to you for free will never satisfy the hunger of a soul that was built to earn its own way.
