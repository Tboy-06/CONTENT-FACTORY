## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Cassian Andor is not a hero of legend, but a mirror reflecting the harsh, unvarnished cost of defiance.
2. His story strips away the mythic glow of the galaxy to reveal the raw, often naive process of radicalization under an iron fist.
3. There are no mystical knights or humming blades here to save the day, only the grit of desperate souls caught in the gears of an empire.
4. This is a ground-level account of revolution, where ordinary people are suddenly thrust into the suffocating reality of an authoritarian takeover.
5. The narrative dissects the terrifying momentum of rising fascism, capturing how systemic oppression forces the common citizen to choose between silence and insurrection.
6. By removing the safety net of Jedi intervention, the audience is left face-to-face with the true, terrifying fragility of freedom.
7. It is a sobering exploration of how a simple scavenger is forged into a catalyst for systemic collapse through trauma and choice.
8. Ultimately, the thousand faces of Cassian Andor prove that real change is not fought with magic, but with the sacrifice of those who have everything to lose.
