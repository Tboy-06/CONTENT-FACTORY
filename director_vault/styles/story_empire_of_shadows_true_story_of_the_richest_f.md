## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. For centuries, the House of Rothschild has wielded a level of global influence that borders on the mythical, shaping the very trajectory of human history through finance and political maneuvering.
2. The dynasty began in the claustrophobic confines of an 18th-century Frankfurt ghetto, where systemic discrimination barred Jewish families from owning land or farming.
3. Denied traditional assets, Mayer Amschel Rothschild turned to the forbidden world of currency and trade, laying the groundwork for a financial empire that would soon span the globe.
4. Through a calculated strategy of familial solidarity, he dispatched his five sons to the great capitals of Europe to establish a networked banking powerhouse.
5. This interconnected web of capital allowed the family to transcend borders, funding sovereign nations and influencing the outcomes of major historical conflicts.
6. They rose from modest roots to become the architects of international finance, mastering the flow of gold and debt during an era of global industrial expansion.
7. Their name became synonymous with unimaginable wealth and secret influence, fueling generations of speculation and complex geopolitical power struggles.
8. The story of the Rothschilds remains a masterclass in how a marginalized family leveraged strategic unity to secure dominance over the modern world.
