## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Across centuries, the House of Rothschild has wielded unprecedented dominance by seamlessly blending familial solidarity with relentless profit-seeking.
2. From modest and constrained roots, they steadily rose to influence international finance, wars, and politics across the globe.
3. They ultimately forged the most formidable and secretive banking dynasty of their age.
4. Yet this sprawling empire of wealth had an entirely unlikely origin within an eighteenth-century ghetto in Frankfurt, Germany.
5. Like many marginalized people of his time, Mayer Amschel Rothschild was legally barred from owning land or farms.
6. Denied traditional avenues of prosperity, he diverted his sharp intellect entirely toward trade, rare coins, and burgeoning financial systems.
7. He strategically positioned his five sons across the major capitals of Europe to create an unrivaled intelligence and capital network.
8. Through resilience and calculated ambition, a restricted ghetto dweller laid the indestructible foundations for the richest family in human history.
