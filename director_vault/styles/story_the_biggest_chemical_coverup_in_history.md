## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 1929, an invisible predator began stalking the living rooms of Chicago, turning ordinary homes into lethal death traps.
2. Families were dropping dead behind closed doors, leaving authorities baffled by a mysterious and silent killer.
3. It took fifteen agonizing deaths before investigators finally unmasked the culprit: the modern household refrigerator.
4. As refrigeration technology evolved, manufacturers abandoned simple ice boxes for chemical cooling systems fueled by methyl chloride.
5. This volatile substance was chosen for its efficiency, yet it functioned as a virtually odorless and highly toxic gas.
6. A single microscopic leak in the copper tubing allowed the poison to seep into the air, suffocating victims while they slept.
7. What began as a domestic convenience quickly transformed into a state-sanctioned nightmare as the industry scrambled to contain the mounting body count.
8. This terrifying era serves as a grim reminder of how unchecked corporate innovation can prioritize progress over the preservation of human life.
