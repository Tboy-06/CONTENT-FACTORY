## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Waking up in a chaotic era with nothing but a life on the run, I suddenly realized my survival depended on a power no one else could see.
2. Trapped by imperial soldiers using my bandit crew for target practice, I faced a choice between certain death and an impossible gamble.
3. My men panicked as the net tightened, but a strange, shifting omen appeared before my eyes, dictating which path promised life and which whispered of calamity.
4. While the others blindly fled toward the fatal trap of the woods, I ignored their desperate pleas and commanded them to charge directly toward the river.
5. The freezing water became our only shield against the arrows raining down, proving that my vision was more than just a hallucination.
6. From that narrow escape, I began to weaponize this eerie foresight to outmaneuver every warlord who crossed my path.
7. Through ruthless tactical genius and the edge of my supernatural gift, I clawed my way from a hunted fugitive to the commander of the entire frontier.
8. Destiny is not written by the powerful, but carved by the survivors who learn to read the signs before the battle even begins.
