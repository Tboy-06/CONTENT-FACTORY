## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. The most controversial debate in documentary filmmaking isn't about ethics, but whether you should ever write a script.
2. Critics often argue that scripting a documentary strips away its authenticity, turning a raw project into manufactured reality television.
3. However, this common misconception ignores the reality of how professional nonfiction storytelling actually functions.
4. In truth, nearly every high-end documentary you watch is built upon a foundation of a detailed script or a structured treatment.
5. A script serves as a roadmap, ensuring your narrative maintains a clear focus amidst hours of sprawling, unorganized footage.
6. By planning your story arc beforehand, you transform chaotic events into a coherent, compelling cinematic experience.
7. Having a vision doesn't invalidate the truth; it provides the necessary framework to actually share that truth with your audience.
8. Ultimately, the best documentaries use scripts not to fake reality, but to guide the viewer through the complexities of it.
