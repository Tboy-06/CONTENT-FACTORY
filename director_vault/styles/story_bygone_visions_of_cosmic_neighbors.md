## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Inciting Anomaly
1. In the spring of 1806, two German astronomers
were independently observing Venus
2. when they noticed something odd.
3. The hemisphere facing away from the Sun
did not perfectly blend with the blackness of space.
4. Instead, it had a faint glow
with a greyish red or ashen grey appearance.
5. This Ashen Light resembled
a phenomenon known as Earthshine.
6. It's when sunlight, reflected by the Earth,
faintly illuminates the nightside of the Moon.
