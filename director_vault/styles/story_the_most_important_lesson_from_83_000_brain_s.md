## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. After analyzing over 83,000 brain scans, Dr. Daniel Amen discovered a single, profound truth that changes how we view human behavior.
2. His journey began far from the medical establishment, rooted in the chaotic environment of being one of seven children.
3. His father famously labeled him a maverick, a term that initially served as a criticism rather than a compliment.
4. The trajectory of his life shifted in 1972 when he was drafted into the army as an infantry medic.
5. It was in the intensity of military service that he discovered his true calling for medicine.
6. However, he quickly realized that his unconventional nature would force him to challenge the status quo of his field.
7. By looking inside the brain rather than just observing external symptoms, he uncovered patterns that others had completely overlooked.
8. Ultimately, the most important lesson from his vast research is that your brain is the hardware of your soul, and caring for it is the most critical investment you can make.
