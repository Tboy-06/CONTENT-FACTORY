## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. You have returned from the field with hours of breathtaking footage and compelling interviews, but now you face the paralyzing reality of a blank editing timeline.
2. The transition from a chaotic mountain of raw files to a cohesive narrative is the most daunting challenge any filmmaker encounters.
3. Instead of getting lost in the visual clutter, you need a streamlined strategy to extract the heart of your story.
4. This is where the power of the text edit becomes your greatest creative asset.
5. A text edit allows you to structure your documentary entirely through transcripts before you ever touch a single frame of video.
6. By focusing on the written word, you can identify your strongest narrative arcs and cut out the fluff without the distraction of visuals.
7. This method transforms your process from trial-and-error assembly into a deliberate and surgical storytelling exercise.
8. Mastering the text edit will save you weeks of frustration and ensure your final film resonates with clarity and purpose.
