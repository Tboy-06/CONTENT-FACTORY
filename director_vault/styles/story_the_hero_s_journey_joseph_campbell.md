## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Embedded deep within the human psyche lies a universal narrative that dictates the shape of every meaningful human story.
2. First identified by mythologist Joseph Campbell, this blueprint is known simply as the hero's journey.
3. This concept transcends ancient mythology to serve as a profound map for personal self-discovery and transformation.
4. It acts as an all-embracing metaphor for the inner trials and triumphs that define a fully realized life.
5. Across every era and culture, heroes embark on this exact same shared path of psychological evolution.
6. Navigating these trials ultimately requires us to confront our deepest fears and embrace profound change.
7. By answering this call to adventure, we unlock the courage needed to shape our own destinies.
8. Ultimately, this transformative path is about learning to follow your bliss.
