## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Our memories define who we are, grounding our past while shaping our ability to navigate the future.
2. Computers mirror this human necessity, acting as digital extensions that rely on their own internal memory to function.
3. Whether it is a feature-length film or a simple text file, every piece of data must be stored and retrieved with precision.
4. At the heart of this process, complex information is broken down into the most fundamental building blocks of digital life: bits.
5. Each individual bit is sequestered within a microscopic memory cell designed to hold a single piece of information.
6. These cells operate like tiny switches, constantly toggling between two distinct physical states to represent binary values.
7. By orchestrating billions of these simple state changes, a computer can execute intricate instructions and sustain our digital world.
8. Ultimately, the essence of all computing power rests on the rapid-fire toggling of these basic units of memory.
