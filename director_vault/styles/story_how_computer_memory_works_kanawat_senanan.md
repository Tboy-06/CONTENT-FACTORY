## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In many ways, our memories make us who we are, helping us remember our past, learn and retain skills, and plan for the future.
2. And for the computers that often act as extensions of ourselves, memory plays much the same role.
3. Whether it is a two-hour movie, a two-word text file, or the instructions for opening either, everything relies on this digital foundation.
4. At its core, everything in a computer's memory takes the form of basic units called bits.
5. Each of these binary digits is securely stored in an individual memory cell.
6. These microscopic cells can effortlessly switch between two distinct states.
7. By toggling back and forth, they represent the two possible values required for computation.
8. Ultimately, this elegant system of switching states allows machines to process the entire digital world.
