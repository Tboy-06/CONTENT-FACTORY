## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. To master the art of storytelling, you must first learn how to break your characters.
2. The legendary Richard Powers argues that true narrative power begins when you push a protagonist to their absolute limit.
3. Great drama is born in the friction of the impossible choice, forcing characters to commit acts they would normally despise.
4. This internal conflict creates the first layer of depth, where a person must face the person they see in the mirror.
5. While we often focus on man against man, the most resonant stories demand that we explore the battle between man and his own principles.
6. However, the true mastery of a story lies in uncovering the hidden third level of drama that transcends individual struggle.
7. If character voice is the engine of your narrative, you must discover the deeper, underlying force that drives the plot forward.
8. Ultimately, the greatest stories are those where the necessity of circumstance strips a character down to their fundamental truth.
