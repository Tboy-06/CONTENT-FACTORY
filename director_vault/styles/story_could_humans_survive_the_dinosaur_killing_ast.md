## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Sixty-six million years ago, a mountain-sized object slammed into Earth, triggering the most catastrophic day in the history of life.
2. The asteroid arrived from the south, striking our planet at three times the speed of a bullet with the force of ten billion atomic bombs.
3. Within mere minutes, the atmosphere ignited, turning the entire surface of the globe into a scorching, suffocating oven.
4. Molten debris rained down from the sky, while massive, impact-induced earthquakes destabilized the tectonic plates beneath our feet.
5. This geological upheaval likely ignited volcanic eruptions across vast distances, effectively choking the atmosphere in a blanket of ash.
6. If humanity had been present, the initial blast alone would have vaporized anyone within thousands of miles of the impact site.
7. Beyond the immediate devastation, the total collapse of the food chain would have turned the survivors' final days into a desperate, losing struggle.
8. Ultimately, the planet’s extreme transformation proves that even a technologically advanced civilization would have been powerless against such a relentless extinction event.
