## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In December 2001, America’s seventh-largest company filed for bankruptcy, vaporizing $63 billion in market value overnight.
2. Thousands of employees were left with nothing, while Wall Street reeled not just from the loss, but from the realization that no one truly understood how it happened.
3. This is the story of Enron, the corporate titan that made billions while effectively selling nothing at all.
4. The collapse was so total and so unexpected that it shattered the public's blind faith in the American financial system.
5. To understand the downfall, you have to look back to 1985, when Enron was simply a boring Houston firm moving natural gas through pipes.
6. The company soon pivoted from a predictable utility provider into a predatory financial machine fueled by complex, fraudulent accounting.
7. By hiding massive debts and inflating earnings, executives created a house of cards that dazzled investors even as the company rotted from within.
8. Enron stands as a haunting reminder that in a world of high-stakes greed, if a profit model seems too magical to be true, it almost certainly is.
