## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Mastering the craft of screenwriting requires navigating a labyrinth of rigid structural demands that separate the amateurs from the masters.
2. Veteran producer and author Dr. Ken Atchity knows these foundational laws better than almost anyone else in the industry.
3. While three rules might seem like an arbitrary limit, they serve as the ultimate litmus test for narrative viability.
4. The absolute first commandment every beginning screenwriter must internalize is that every single element must interconnect seamlessly.
5. This obsessive interconnectedness forms the defining boundary line between the sprawling freedom of a novel and the tight constraints of a script.
6. Novels easily afford authors the luxury of wandering off on tangents that never truly converge by the final chapter.
7. Screenplays, however, punish narrative drift with instant rejection because every scene must aggressively drive the central engine forward.
8. If an idea on page one fails to cast a shadow on page 158, it has no business surviving the final draft.
