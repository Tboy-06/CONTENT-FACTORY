## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. In 1883, a man was born whose life’s work would eventually unlock the secret code to extraordinary wealth.
2. Napoleon Hill spent decades dissecting the lives of the world’s most powerful titans, from John D. Rockefeller to Henry Ford.
3. He obsessed over one burning question: why do these few individuals command unimaginable fortune while the masses struggle to survive?
4. Hill embarked on a rigorous investigation, documenting the specific psychological frameworks that propelled these legends to the top.
5. The result was Think and Grow Rich, a blueprint for success that transformed into the definitive manual on personal wealth.
6. By analyzing the habits of pioneers like Thomas Edison and Alexander Graham Bell, Hill distilled success into thirteen actionable principles.
7. These are not merely theories but time-tested laws of achievement that bridged the gap between average thinkers and world-class visionaries.
8. Mastering these thirteen core principles remains the most reliable path to achieving your own financial independence and lasting legacy.
