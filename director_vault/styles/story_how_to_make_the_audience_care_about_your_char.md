# Story Structure: How To Make The Audience Care About Your Characters by John Truby

## Narrative Overview
- Format: 8-Sentence Short
- Total Duration: 172.0s
- Hook: "Why do audiences walk away from visually stunning films without feeling a single thing for the protagonist?"
- Payoff: "When a character pursues a goal with genuine human struggle, the audience stops watching and starts feeling."

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Why do audiences walk away from visually stunning films without feeling a single thing for the protagonist?
2. We have all sat through high-budget spectacles that fall flat because the core human connection is entirely missing.
3. Great production value is no substitute for a character who truly resonates with the viewer.
4. Most screenwriters believe they must force the audience to like their hero from the very first frame.
5. John Truby argues that this common obsession with being likable is actually a fundamental mistake.
6. True emotional investment does not come from perfection or surface-level charm.
7. Instead, you must build a character through authentic choices that reveal their deepest inner needs.
8. When a character pursues a goal with genuine human struggle, the audience stops watching and starts feeling.
