## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Your brain is a survival machine, engineered by evolution to make snap judgments that keep you alive.
2. Imagine an early human who encounters a lion for the first time and tragically mistakes the predator for a harmless playmate.
3. That fatal error is quickly filtered out of the gene pool, leaving behind only the ancestors who learned to run on instinct.
4. Fast forward five years, and that same ancestor spots a silhouette in the distance and immediately chooses to hide rather than investigate.
5. This lightning-fast reaction happens without a moment of deep, conscious thought because your survival depends on speed over accuracy.
6. Daniel Kahneman describes this as System 1, the impulsive, automatic, and intuitive engine of your mind.
7. While this system saved our ancestors from lions, it often leads to cognitive biases that cloud our judgment in the modern world.
8. To navigate life effectively, you must learn when to bypass these instant reactions and engage the slower, more logical System 2.
