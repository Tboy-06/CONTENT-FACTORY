5. Through expressive camera work, vibrant color palettes, and intimate lighting, Jenkins transforms unspoken emotion into cinematic poetry.
6. Every symbolic motif—from the surrounding ocean water to the evolving color tones—reflects Chiron's agonizing search for human connection.
