## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Every once in a while, a revolutionary product arrives to permanently shift the trajectory of human history.
2. When Steve Jobs stepped onto that stage in 2007, he promised to reinvent the phone, but few realized the chaos unfolding behind the scenes.
3. The industry was already saturated with titans, leaving skeptics to wonder why Apple would dare enter such a volatile market.
4. The internal truth was simpler and far more personal: the existing handsets were fundamentally frustrating to use.
5. Engineers faced the impossible task of shrinking a computer, an iPod, and a phone into a single, seamless device.
6. Prototypes were failing, software was unstable, and the vision seemed to be slipping through their fingers just weeks before the launch.
7. Against all odds, the project transcended its technical limitations to ignite a global digital transformation.
8. With over two billion units sold, the iPhone solidified its legacy as the most influential invention of the modern era.
