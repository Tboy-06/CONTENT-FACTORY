## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Long before Mad Men redefined prestige television, the concept began as a mysterious urge to capture the 1960s.
2. Navigating the early days of the internet made historical research difficult, forcing creator Matthew Weiner to hire a specialized researcher.
3. The first crucial document to return outlined the intense 1960 crisis threatening the tobacco advertising industry.
4. But historical events were merely the backdrop for a far more personal story brewing beneath the surface.
5. Weiner wanted to write about a thirty-five-year-old man who seemingly had everything, yet suffered from an overwhelming internal misery.
6. He channeled his own existential dread into a slick, secretive advertising executive named Don Draper.
7. At the time, no one truly understood who this mysterious protagonist was or where his journey would lead.
8. Yet it was precisely that unanswered enigma of human discontent that birthed a modern storytelling masterpiece.
