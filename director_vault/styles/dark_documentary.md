# Dark Documentary Style
Tags: #style #cinematic #noir #vox #johnny_harris

## Visual Guidelines
- Color Palette: Deep slate/black (#0B0F19), stark white text, cyan & amber accents.
- Image Treatment: High-contrast B&W archival grain with smooth cosine push-in zoompan.
- Cadence: Rapid cut transitions every 2.2 to 2.8 seconds.
- Primary Hooks: [[hooks/visual_evidence_first]], [[hooks/negative_curiosity_gap]]
- SFX Palette: [[sfx/whoosh]], [[sfx/thud]], [[sfx/riser]]
