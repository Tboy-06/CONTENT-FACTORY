# Story Structure: Every Dinosaur Period Explained

- Total Duration: 601.0s

## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. Imagine a world where ninety-six percent of all marine life vanished in a geological instant.
2. Two hundred and fifty-two million years ago, the catastrophic Permian-Triassic extinction event devastated our planet.
3. Known universally as the Great Dying, this cataclysm also wiped out seventy percent of terrestrial vertebrate species.
4. Colossal, prolonged volcanic eruptions tore through the Siberian Traps in modern-day Russia.
5. These relentless eruptions unleashed astronomical quantities of greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.
6. A runaway greenhouse effect rapidly triggered extreme and irreversible climate change across the globe.
7. Earth was left thoroughly depleted, its scarred landscapes reduced to a single, barren supercontinent.
8. From this apocalyptic wasteland, the dawn of the Triassic period forced the genesis of a brand new world.
