## Acts & Narrative Breakdown
### Act 1: Full Narrative Arc
1. After four years of making films, I finally discovered a single technique that completely changed my storytelling game.
2. I was genuinely embarrassed that I hadn't realized this fundamental rule of narrative structure sooner.
3. The secret lies in understanding that every scene you create must serve one of two purposes.
4. A scene is either there to drive the plot forward or to express the internal emotion of your characters.
5. The most common mistake creators make is stacking too many scenes of the same type together.
6. If you barrage your audience with nothing but plot, you lose their emotional connection to the story.
7. Conversely, overloading a sequence with constant emotion makes the narrative feel stagnant and directionless.
8. By intentionally alternating between plot and emotion, you create the perfect rhythm for a truly compelling story.
