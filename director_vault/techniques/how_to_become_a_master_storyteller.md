## Top Viewer Feedback
> "The Dance (0:24–3:27): Connect story beats with “but” or “therefore” instead of "and then" to build tension.

Rhythm (3:27–5:20): Alternate between short, medium, and long sentences to create a musical, engaging flow.

Tone (5:20–6:57): Write and film as if you are talking to one specific close friend to sound conversational.

Direction (6:57–8:05): Define your final, memorable line first, then work backward to structure the middle content.

Story-lenses (8:05–9:17): Apply a unique perspective or angle to common topics to differentiate your content.

The Hook (9:17–10:49): Start with a punchy, plot-indicative sentence and use immediate on-screen visuals to retain viewers." (at 0:24) — @wesfin
