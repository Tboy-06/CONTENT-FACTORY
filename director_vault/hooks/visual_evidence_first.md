# Visual Evidence First
Tags: #hook #formula #high_retention

## Directing Principle
Always show the raw document, photograph, or map BEFORE explaining context.
- Associated Style: [[styles/dark_documentary]]
- Transition Sound: [[sfx/whoosh]]

## Proven Hook Examples
- "This airport is Texas Army. Look at this."
- "Declassified in 2021, this single page changes the official story."
