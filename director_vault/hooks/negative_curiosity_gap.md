# Negative Curiosity Gap
Tags: #hook #suspense #psychology

## Directing Principle
Confront the viewer with a hidden mistake, danger, or forbidden truth.
- Associated Style: [[styles/dark_documentary]]
- Transition Sound: [[sfx/thud]]

## Proven Hook Examples
- "Why 99% of people never build wealth until it's too late."
- "You use this app every day, but its real business model will terrify you."
